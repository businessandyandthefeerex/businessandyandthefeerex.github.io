Pork belly with artichoke, apple puree and pomegranate  
Mushroom croquettes  
Roast potatoes  
Sticky date pudding with candied walnuts and ice cream  
Almond and orange polenta cake with berry coulis and ice cream  

Back at Tongariro Lodge on our annual Labour weekend trip to Turangi, this time we decided focus on starters, sides and desserts rather than mains. 

The mushroom croquettes were controversial. Business Andy thought they were great. The Fee-Rex thought the flavour was a great example of why some people don’t like mushrooms. It was pungent, almost foot-like. The pork belly had an interesting mix of flavours, but one end of the belly was very dry. And the roast potatoes were good enough, but nothing for which you should go out of your way.

Berry coulis is delicious. But you can’t just put it anywhere. It’s a strong flavour, too strong to go with the almond and orange cake, it just stomped all over it and didn’t really pair well. The sticky date pudding, however, was excellent. Mad props to the candied walnuts, where our only complaint was that there weren’t more of them. 

Overall we think the Lodge have done enough to maintain their three status, but they do need to treat lightly.
