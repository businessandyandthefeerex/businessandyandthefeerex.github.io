What a shame, a real missed opportunity there. The Offering had a lot of promise, but fell short both of our expectations and their potential. 

They had done well in what we would consider to be some of the more difficult facets of running a cafe. Their menu was varied and interesting, they nailed the chips, and the flavours of the tacos and also of the burger were excellent. But they had toasted the pretzel burger bun to such a degree that you almost had to snap it in half, and the tacos had been sitting around, waiting to be delivered for so long by the time we got them, they had gone cold. That's basic stuff, and it's reflected in their score. 

They also didn't make a particularly good espresso martini. In fact, we'd say they made quite a bad espresso martini. Maybe they were just looking out for our road safety, but it really seemed like it was just half a bottle of cold brew poured into a glass.
