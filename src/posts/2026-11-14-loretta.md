Loretta offers Mediterranean tapas style dining and is bloody good. The flatbread was soft and hot, with ample whipped ricotta that was so tasty we would have been happy to just munch it all up with a spoon. The pizza was similarly on point. Only the koftas were imperfect, with one large piece of gristle in one and scalding hot metal skewers, so hot that it was weird that they didn't say anything. Other than that blemish, this was a great night of food.
