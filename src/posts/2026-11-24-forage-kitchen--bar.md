Forage Kitchen + Bar will be disappointed with their rating. In fairness, no restaurant wants the dreaded "meh" of 2-dom. But for establishments with highfalutin ideas and larger price tags it is particularly offensive. In this case, it was well deserved. The food was a mixed bag, which we'll get to, but the real problem was the service. Or lack thereof. We had to beg at every turn. Please take our drinks order. Please don't look snippily at us when we don't also have our food order ready at the same time, we've been too dehydrated to peruse the menu. Please may we order our food. Please may we have some fresh side plates. For a restaurant with only a maximum of 4 tables dining at any one time, and two servers on the floor, this was abysmal. The whole experience took over 2 hours, and that's with us chivvying them along. [Dragonfly](/posts/2026-02-03-dragonfly.md) would have been able to get that service done in 15 minutes flat. 

We are fans of the 'just feed me' menu. Inserting yet more randomness into our dining experiences, sign us up! But this one was perplexing. Their online menu isn't working so we can't precisely include the wording, but it said something like "6 dish". In most just feed me's that means 6 little courses. We ordered (with Business Andy's mother) two non-vegetarian and one vegetarian meals. This resulted in three different entrees, three different mains, one salad, and two desserts aka 9 dishes. How is this 6 dishes? What would have happened if there were four of us? The whole thing was confusing, and left you with the unsettling feeling of not knowing the structure of the meal you were "enjoying". 

As to the food, there were some stars in the bunch. The lamb rump was juicy and the jus had a pleasantly surprising spicy kick. The tiramisu wasn't innovative, but a good example of the genre which is just fine by us. The nduja fish, which we were frankly frightened of, was bloody nice, tasting much more like nduja than fish. But they were stingy on the bread, we could have happily had double the amount provided for the amount of fish smush. 

The cauliflower sounded exciting on the menu, in fact the description contained the word "pie". It was not in any way a pie. It was baked bits of cauliflower, not so baked bits of cauliflower, a type of white foam, and rather a lot of salad. The carrot cake on the creme brulee was dehydrated to the point of shattering. It was served in chunks too large for a comfortable mouthful, especially when there were only two chunks between three people sharing, but God help you when you go to try and split it with your spoon. Wear protective goggles. The cabbage salad was watery and became a chore to get through. The other dishes fell between the top and the bottom.
