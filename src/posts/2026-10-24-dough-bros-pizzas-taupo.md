Chicken burger  
Meatball folded bombshell  

Let’s attend to the obvious question first - what is a folded bombshell? Perhaps a calzone? That’s what we thought when we ordered it. In fact, a folded bombshell is a pizza base, with toppings on one half, and the other half folded over the top, but not sealed. Kind of like an open calzone. It’s a curious invention, and we’re not entirely sure what it’s bringing to the occasion, but it’s always nice to have something new. The meatballs were impractical. Four or so large meatballs across half a pizza means in reality you either have to have a few bites that are mostly meatball, or you need to smush your meatball up at which point it isn’t a meatball anymore. Small meatballs, or pre-smashed meatballs (aka mince) would have been better. The flavours were great though, a little bit spicy, and a herbalicious base. 

The burger was spectacular. We are ambivalent about the brioche bun - a few years ago everybody suddenly decided that brioche was the only burger bun worth having, which we just didn’t agree with. But this one was out of this world, and is the only one worth having. The softness was inspirational, your fingers were indenting the bun like eating a marshmallow. But it still had the structural integrity to hold up throughout, even through the challenging transfer from person to person. The chicken was crispy and fresh, the salads balanced, the sauce just right in flavour and amount. Keep ‘em coming Dough Bros.
