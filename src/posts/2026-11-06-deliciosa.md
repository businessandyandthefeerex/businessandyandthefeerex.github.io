Delicioso knows good food. Their menu was packed with good looking options, and the tapas style gives you freedom to try a variety. 

The standout was the brisket. That bad boy was juicy and tender, served on yummy potatoes and a standout salsa. Chickpeas, beans and corn are three of The Fee-Rex’s least favourite ingredients, but you wouldn’t know it with the way she housed that salsa, it was truly a triumph. 

Also triumphant was the abnormally good flatbread. It wasn’t very flat, twas puffy and delicious with an incredible spread, a little spicy and very generously portioned. The arancini balls were herby and appealing, but not quite in the same league as the other two dishes which were out of this world. 

Two criticisms to close. Firstly, sort out your air con. Thanks to a poorly placed heat pump, one half of the dining room was chilly, the other half (where we sat) too hot. Secondly, sort out your cocktail consistency. At our table we ordered two cocktails twice. Each time the second cocktail was significantly different to the first - clearly made by two different people following two different recipes. Most egregious was the coconut mojito which came in two very different sizes which completely threw out all the ratios.
