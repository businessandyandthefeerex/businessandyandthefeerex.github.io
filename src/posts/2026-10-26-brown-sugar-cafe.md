Savoury scone  
Pesto danish  
Quince and custard cake  

Brown Sugar Cafe is a Taihape staple, as the line out the door at lunchtime on Labour Day attested. We really wanted to like it. Unfortunately, while the staff were friendly and the service was prompt and professional, they just didn’t quite deliver on the food. 

The pesto danish sounded delicious, but was a letdown with an oily texture and less flavour than we were expecting. The scone was better, we appreciated the variety of veg making an appearance while not overwhelming the scone, and the generous but not excessive butter portion. But again the flavour was a bit of a fail - perhaps we needed a herb or a spice in play? 

The strongest offering was the cake. Firstly, kudos on doing something different in the cake space, we have never had a quince and custard cake before. Secondly, double kudos for having a tonne of custard in that cake. And the quince bought an interesting flavour too, with the yoghurt being a welcome accompaniment. Unfortunately, the cake was on the dry side. They got away with it a bit because of the amount of custard and presence of yoghurt, but it was still noted.
