That's twice this week that we've been let down on service. We hate to see it. 

Apache, we want to see you excel. Food wise, you were great, debate could be had about whether your food was deserving of a four. But with service like that, you just had no chance. In fairness, all of your servers were very friendly and personable. We loved how your bar staff told us about how she'd just broken a nail, her nails were still on fleek, by the way. We want you to be personal with us. Unfortunately, most of the time we felt ignored. It took ages to bring us water, so long that we got our water and ordered our drinks and food at the same time as it had been at least ten minutes since we'd sat down and frankly we were scared you weren't coming back.

The glasses from our first round of drinks was still sitting on the table when we left, as was an empty bottle of water. These are damning signs. Worse still, at the end of the meal you asked if we were interested in dessert. Why yes, we wouldn't mind a squizz at the dessert menu, we replied. And squizz we did. We selected a really interesting sounding dessert to share. We put the menu to the side in the universal ready-to-order position. Then we waited. And waited. We drank our second cocktails, which were almost full when we started perusing the dessert menu. No service came, and we left without dessert. You're leaving money on the table. And while they were busy, they weren't so busy that they didn't have time to take our order, especially by dessert time. 

Enough of that, let's praise some food. Everything was good, and some were great. We both agreed the spring rolls were the highlight, nailing it on texture and flavour with an interesting dipping sauce. The salad was also top tier, and eggplant is not always an easy ingredient to pull off. The tofu was delish, but uneven in the distribution of gochujang sauce. And finally, the dumplings soared, but not quite as high as the other dishes.
