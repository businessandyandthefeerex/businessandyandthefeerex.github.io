Smorgasbord: pork wontons, steamed brocolli, vegetarian spring rolls, vegetarian samosas, fried noodles, sweet & sour pork, black bean pork, egg foo yong, prawn crackers

 If you find yourself needing to eat at The Plaza food court, we do not recommend Red Lantern. The steamed broccoli was the only item that wasn't bad, and this was probably only because we saw them bring it out fresh in front of us. The low points were numerous and deep, with joint honours going to the spring rolls and the fried noodles, which were cold. Just because you are a food court Chinese smorgasbord does not excuse the blatant lack of effort and care shown at Red Lantern. If you choose to subject yourself to Red Lantern, make sure you order a drink or you will suffer the icy cold displeasure of the counter staff.
