Palmerston North



We at lunch here again in November 2021



Fried chicken and waffles and maple syrup

Cheeseburger spring roll with coleslaw

Basque cheesecake



What a huge let down this was. We came in so excited, but it only one short week the magic had faded. This was still a good meal, it just wasn't the great meal that we expected. 



The fried chicken was pretty good, but the waffles that came with it seemed like they came out of single plastic sleeves at a supermarket. They looked really interesting as they were fingers of waffle instead regular flat waffles, but they were doughy, thick, and flavourless. There wasn't enough maple syrup, and it was just missing something, like creme fraiche or, we don't know, just something. 



The spring roll was really tasty. It was super cool that it was just like a cheeseburger, complete with pickles, beef mince, cheese, and mustard. However some of the details were a little sloppy. The coleslaw was naked (nobody wants to eat plain raw cabbage) and the spring roll wasn't quite hot, it was only warmed through. 



The cheesecake divided us, Business Andy really liked it, but The Fee-Rex thought it was too plain and eggy. Some people might say that you should expect that from a baked cheesecake, but those people liked the Basque cheesecake. And where was the yoghurt or cream? 



All together disappointing, perhaps because they were right up the top, there's only one way to go from there. We still want to go back because this place has such promise. Better luck next time.

We ate lunch here in November 2021



Creamy mushrooms on ciabatta

Tan square

Savoury muffin



The person who made the food at Boho Cafe loves food. And we love them. Everything was delicious, and Boho Cafe had taken extra steps to make eating there a pleasure. 



The mushrooms were exceptional, the addition of caramelised onion really elevated the dish. They had thoughtfully put the mushrooms and sauce in their own bowl so that the toast didn't get soggy, and unlike literally every other cafe on the planet, their butter wasn't fresh out of the fridge and rock solid. The savoury muffin was a little unusual as it featured leek, but was soft, fluffy, and fresh, and had just the right amount of salty feta on top. The caramel to base ratio of the tan square was just right, and the caramel itself wasn't overpoweringly sweet.



To nitpick, there wasn't enough toast with the mushrooms, there was a hair in the creamy sauce, and they didn't bring us cake forks for the tan square, so we had to use the mushroom forks. This is still an exceptionally good cafe though, and we are going again next week. Bookings recommended.
