We ate dinner here in July 2021



Vegetable gyoza

Chicken karaage

Inari sushi

Miso white fish

Beef kushiage

Takoyaki

Lemon panna cotta with vanilla ice cream



Another enjoyable experience at Yatai. We were surprised by the miso white fish, which doesn't sound like much, but came with a delicious yuzu and miso sauce. It was the best dish of the night. We wouldn't order the inari sushi again, it was too sweet and a bit uninteresting. Everything else was good to great, including the panna cotta, actually, which was really lemon-ey, and came with a really nice vanilla ice cream. Bookings highly recommended.

We ate dinner here in March 2021



Chicken karaage

Pork gyoza

Shiitake age

Potato croquettes



Yatai is a little bit out of the way, but it's worth stepping off the main drag to go here. The plates are shared, sort of like tapas style, and the restaurant recommends two plates per person which seems about right. Everything was just delicious. All the meals apart from the gyoza came with shredded cabbage on the side, which at first we thought was just a garnish, but truly elevated each dish. Our waitress did forget about us for a bit, but that didn't bring us down, and the service on the whole was friendly. Bookings recommended.
