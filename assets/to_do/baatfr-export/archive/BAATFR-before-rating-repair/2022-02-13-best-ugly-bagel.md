Wellington Airport



We ate breakfast here in July 2022 



Swiss melt bagel: Cheese and marmite 



As far as cheese and marmite bagels go, this was okay. The uneven cut of the bagel meant there was a more-grilled and a less-grilled half. The Swiss Melt was better than the Jellytip, but worse than the Yodi (see below). How much marmite to put on is always a controversial subject. Best Ugly Bagels has gone for a middle-of-the-road, about 6/10 marmite coverage. The Fee-Rex could have done with a little less, but then she generally prefers a thin smear. Business Andy found it about right. Perhaps the Swiss Melt bagel is just not made to be shared. 



-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



We ate lunch here in February 2022



Yodi bagel: pastrami, Swiss cheese, mustard, pickle

Jellytip bagel: chocolate, raspberry jam, whatever that white stuff was



While they did their best to emulate the venerable jelly tip ice cream, Best Ugly Bagel fell a bit short here. The chocolate sauce wasn't particularly nice, the jam was too sweet, and the chocolate bagel was a mistake, they should have just gone for a plain bagel here and let the toppings do the talking.



The yodi bagel was quite a bit nicer, our only real complaint is that it was a bit too oily. Business Andy, a true pickle maniac, would have preferred a bit more pickle, but he says that about everything.
