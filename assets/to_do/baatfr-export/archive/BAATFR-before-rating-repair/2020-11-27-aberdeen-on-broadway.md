We ate dinner here in March 2021



Bruschetta 

Lamb rack with basil pesto crust, potato gratin, carrot, and courgette

Chicken Maryland with polenta and slaw

Double chocolate bavarois with mint ice cream



Another very good meal. The best part was the caramel espresso martini, it was one of the best cocktails that we've ever had. Also of note, was that the bread in the bruschetta was hot, why isn't everyone else doing this? The bavarois was a total knockout too. The lamb was great, but the chicken was a bit forgettable. Bookings essential.

We ate dinner here in November 2020



Eye fillet with potato gratin, blue cheese sauce, red wine jus, baby carrot, and green beans

Venison with blue cheese and pear tart, fig and honey jus, baby carrot, and broccoli 

Lemon cheesecake



What a sensational meal, everything was delicious, in particular, the blue cheese and pear tart stood out. Excellent cocktail menu, and they weren't tight-fisted with the portion sizes either. Honestly we both expected tiny meals, and to have to then get sides as extras, but this is not that kind of establishment. Bookings essential.
