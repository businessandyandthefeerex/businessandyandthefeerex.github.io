Palmerston North



We ate dinner here in January 2022 



Pulled pork tacos 

Beef enchiladas 

Churros with caramel sauce and ice cream 



Libre is Palmerston North's premiere Mexican eatery, with a nice casual vibe and great cocktails. The food was good to excellent, with the tacos being the highlight. Have your serviette at the ready, you will get sauced. The churros were delicious too, but they were just churros and could have been a little more fluffy and a little more inventive. 

Bookings recommended
