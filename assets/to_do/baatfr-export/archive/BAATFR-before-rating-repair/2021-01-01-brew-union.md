We ate lunch here in October 2021



Philly cheese steak burger with chips

Lamb rack with potato and pumpkin gratin, pea puree, and yoghurt harissa sauce

Ginger kiss cocktail

Salted caramel martini



The cocktails continue to impress, the ginger kiss was a little different this time around, we think they had added limoncello, and it wasn't quite as good as we remembered. The lamb rack was perfectly cooked, and the gratin was delicious, it really stood out. It was unique, they had layered the potato and pumpkin together in the one gratin, yum yum. The Philly cheese steak burger wasn't quite as good as the other burgers that we've had here, but was still very strong, and there wasn't too much cheese in it. 



----------------------------------------------------------



We ate dinner here again in January 2021 



Cheesy woodfired garlic bread with balsamic vinegar and pesto 

Calamari with passionfruit sweet chilli dipping sauce 

Scotch fillet, kumara mash, spinach, stout jus 

Fried chicken burger with chips and aioli 

Swamp Juice hazy IPA 



Another excellent meal at Brew Union. The garlic bread was something else, and much more of a pizza bread than a normal loaf. We were pleasantly surprised by the passionfruit sauce that came with the calamari, and the calamari itself was also delicious and not chewy at all. The fried chicken burger was superior in taste but inferior in structural integrity to the beef burger we had on the previous visit. The steak paled in comparison to the other meals but was still good. Last time we were here, we were remiss in not trying a beer. The Swamp Juice was fruity and hoppy - a good beer.

We ate dinner here in December 2020



Fried cauliflower 

Beef burger and chips

Pumpkin stuffed with ricotta, spinach, sundried tomatoes, and rice



Great cocktail list, we had four different cocktails between us and could have tried more. The fried cauliflower was delish, as was the burger, if a bit salty. The pumpkin came with a warning: "this is a lot of pumpkin" and while this is true, it's not a bad thing.
