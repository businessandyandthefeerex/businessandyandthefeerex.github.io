We ate dinner here in December 2021 



Blue cheese and walnut flatbread 

Steak espetada with carrot puree, brocollini, tomato and potatoes 

Kumara, chickpea, lentil and lamb stew with corn rice and yoghurt 

Waffle with cream, vanilla ice cream and maple syrup 



Sorry Little Savannah, but you've fallen from your pedestal. We let you squeak by last time and retain your 4, but some insults will not stand. The stew was such an insult. The meat was dry and the stew was bland. Also, it was served in a cast iron pot, with a separate plate for the rice and yoghurt. This meant the stew was thermonuclear and stayed that way, while juggling between the pot and the plate was awkward. A standard bowl or plate would have been better. The flatbread was excellent as previous, as was the espetada. The waffle was good but not great, just missing that little something to make it special.

We ate lunch here in May 2021



Beef burger with onion rings and chips

Chicken espatada with béarnaise sauce, chips, and onion rings



This was still a good meal, and is still a 4, but the magic is starting to wear off here. The chicken was juicy and flavoursome, although there was one piece of gristle, and the burger was absolutely fantastic. The biggest issue was the onion rings, Little Savannah used to serve giant deep fried onion rings that they made themselves, imagine our disappointment when crumbed onion rings that looked  they came from a bulk catering pack arrived at our table. They didn't even have real onion inside. Do you know who serves onion rings like that? Burger King. We would have rather had no onion rings. In other bad news, the Fee-Rex was given chips after ordering roast potato on the side, and the béarnaise was unusually foamy. These are rookie mistakes, and when you are at the top, you have to keep performing, there's no room to rest on your laurels. Bookings essential.

We ate dinner here in March 2021



Crumbed goats' cheese balls

BBQ pork belly flatbread

Beef burger with added blue cheese butter and cheese, chips and onion rings 

Pork belly, pumpkin and potato mash, cabbage and bacon



Little Savannah continues to impress, although perhaps not quite to the level of our lunch last year. The pork belly flatbread was delicious, especially the generous amounts of crackling. The burger was tasty, but juicy to the point of being wet causing severe bun deterioration. The cabbage and bacon accompanying the pork belly was delicious and there was a considerable amount of crackling, although the meat itself and the mash were a bit plain.  Bookings still essential.

We ate lunch here in November 2020



Crumbed goats' cheese balls

Blue cheese and walnut flatbread

Onion rings

Boerewors roll with with caramelized onion, mustard, and chips

Eye fillet with Béarnaise sauce, broccoli, tomato, potatoes, and onion rings



Hands down the best Piña Colada that either of us has ever had, the steak was delicious although it wasn't improved by the tomato, the boerewors was delicious if a little basic, it's like a hotdog turned up to 11. Bookings essential.
