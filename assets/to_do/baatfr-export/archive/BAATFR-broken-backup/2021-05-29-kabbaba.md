We ate lunch here in May 2021



Lamb iskender with sweet chilli, hummus, and mayonnaise

Lamb doner with hot chilli, satay, and yoghurt



How dare you charge extra for satay sauce and garlic aioli? These are standard kebab sauces and should carry no extra charge over any of the other sauces. News flash, garlic aioli is basically just mayo. Maybe this was the source of the three customer complaints that we witnessed in the short time we were there, or maybe it was the absurdly long wait between ordering and eating. To be fair, the Plaza was positively heaving with people on the day we were there, so they were perhaps a lot busier than they expected. The food itself was acceptable. The doner was just the right size to actually fit in your mouth, and consequently was the right size for lunch. In contrast, the iskender was enormous, no Turkish bread came with it though. So aside from the busy food court setting, the angry customers, and the expensive sauces, Kabbaba just blends into the background hum of every other ordinary Turkish establishment.
