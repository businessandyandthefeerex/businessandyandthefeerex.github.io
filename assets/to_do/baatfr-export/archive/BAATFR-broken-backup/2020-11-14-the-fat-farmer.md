We ate dinner here again in February 2025



Fried goat cheese with honey and hazelnuts

Bruschetta

Chips with Joseph spices and sumac

Fried chicken with gochujang sesame glaze and kewpie mayo

Fried pork bao buns with pickled cucumber and peanut hoisin sauce

Roll your own duck pancakes with cucumber and peanut and hoisin sour cream

Sticky date pudding with spiced ice cream and brandy snap



 Fourth time on the bounce, Fat Farmer congratulations you've done it again. Another excellent meal. And yes, we didn't get anything we hadn't had before (except the chips), but to be fair there weren't a lot of other options on the menu. There were some changes, reviewing over time certain items had drifted in and out, but this isn't a menu which changes cataclysmically with the seasons. 



We will note that the wait time between the third and fourth wave of food was uncomfortably long, but then again our stomachs probably appreciated a bit of down time. The fried goat cheese, which we were having for a third time, continues to be the best item on the menu, with an honourable mention for the bruschetta. The weakest offering was duck pancakes, which were just a little ho hum compared to the rest.

We ate dinner here again in July 2022



Goats cheese with honey and hazlenuts

Polenta crumbed squid rings with mayo 

Chicken bao buns

Pork bao buns

Gnocchi with pumpkin, sage and burnt butter 

Chicken thigh with Israeli couscous 

Tarte tartin with cream and mascarpone 



Fat Farmer, you've done it again. At present, this is our favourite place to eat in Palmy. Tonight we had a few new dishes, and a few we'd had before. The goats cheese continues to be outstanding. We've had both bao buns before, and tonight we reaffirmed that the pork is indeed better than the chicken. 



The squid rings, ooh lah lah. It was salty, but not too salty. And talk about tender. The gnocchi was scrumptious. The Fee-Rex loves to order gnocchi then say that it's gross - but not tonight! Pan fried to perfection, the pumpkin a perfect accompaniment, and the burnt butter sauce was extra. The chicken was the weakest link. It wasn't bad, but when surrounded by these giants of deliciousness, it paled in comparison. The tarte tartin was ginormous and bloody nice. We were surprised by how large it was and took half home. We didn't spring for the ice cream on the side, and this was a mistake. As always, the cocktails were also great. 



Bookings still essential.

We at dinner here in November 2021



Bruschetta

Beef cheek with waffles

Fried chicken

Flat iron steak with coffee rub



This was the strongest showing from the Fat Farmer thus far, which is saying something given how good their meals have been in the past. Everything that came out of the kitchen was amazing, and yet somehow, the beef cheek still managed to stand out. The cocktails were a bit average but this was an absolutely fabulous meal. Bookings essential.

We ate dinner here again in December 2020



Duck pancakes with hoisin sauce

Pan fried goats' cheese, honey hazlenuts, toasted bread

Bread sticks with hummus

Pork belly bao buns with peanut hoisin sauce

Chocolate and cherry terrine



The Fat Farmer continues to impress. The goats' cheese was just as good the second time around. The duck pancakes were tasty and fun to assemble. The bread sticks that came with the hummus were not what we expected when we ordered "hummus with toasted bread" and were a bit dry and bland, the hummus was difficult to appreciate because of this. The pork belly buns were even better than the chicken ones that we had last time we were here. And finally, the terrine was very rich, but nice, it would have been a bit much to eat by oneself, we shared it between three. Bookings still essential.

We ate dinner here in November 2020



Baked cauliflower, almonds & pomegranate 

Pan fried goats' cheese, honey hazlenuts, toasted bread

Crispy chicken bao buns

Sticky date pudding with ice cream.



It's not your traditional, sit down, order 3 courses, food arrives kind of restaurant. The food just comes out when it's done (except the dessert) and is great for sharing, a bit like tapas. The goats' cheese was the highlight, bookings essential.
