Wellington



We ate lunch here in April 2023



Pita bread and hummus

Fondue and pretzels

Salt and pepper squid 

Polenta chips

Korean fried chicken



Last time we ate here, we described the service as "Capital S Speedy". Something has changed, as this time we waited 20 minutes for four drinks and the restaurant was <20% full. 



The fondue was sensational. Plenty of it, warm, good texture, with a wee kick of spice. The accompanying pretzels were pretty good, although describing them as 'giant' was an over-statement. Also, the whole thing was a rip off considering separately on the menu you could purchase just the bowl of fondue for $4, which means we were paying $18 for two medium sized pretzels and some salad. Nonetheless, it was the tastiest thing we ate. 



A close second was the pita bread and hummus. Unlike at Parrotdog, the pitas weren't overcooked and the hummus was flavourful and delicious. Also doing fairly well was the Korean fried chicken. It was spicy and juicy, and hit the notes you'd expect. Could've done with more dipping sauce. 



At the bottom of the list was the squid and the polenta chips. We love polenta chips, but these ones were dry as a bone. The squid was bizarre. It wasn't rings, instead it almost looked like a chopped up churro. It was batter with a stick of squid through the middle. It was chewy. And the flavouring wasn't salt-and-peppery either.

We ate dinner here in December 2021



Pork belly bites

Haloumi fries

Pan fried fish with asparagus, Anna potatoes, and lemon caper butter

Lamb shoulder with kumara mash, broccolini, and port wine jus



The service at Southern Cross is capital S Speedy. The entrees came out so fast that we assumed that there had been a mistake. The pork belly bites were tasty, but could have been better rendered, and the haloumi fries were delicious but came with an underwhelming sauce. They really did come out fast though.



The mains also came out absurdly quickly. The fish was meant to come with beans, and it was an unwelcome surprise when asparagus showed up. It's not that there was anything in particular wrong with the asparagus, but the Fee-Rex is not a fan, and doesn't think that restaurants can assume that it's okay to swap one green thing with another without warning. Business Andy absolutely loved the little crispy fried capers that came on the side, and although they weren't on the menu either, they were a much more welcome surprise. The fish itself was so-so, and the potato was pretty good. 



The lamb dish was really good, the jus did a lot of work, and went with everything. It's hard to go wrong with lamb, broccolini, and kumara mash, although it didn't appear to have been roasted as was stated on the menu.
