We ate dinner here again in September 2024



Banquet:

Labneh

Hummus with chickpeas

Pickled vegetables

Bean salad

Coleslaw

Olives

Roast potatoes

Dry fried cauliflower

Lamb shoulder

Chicken leg



A damn shame to see a former 4 fall down to a 3 like this. But it's not their first time falling down like this. Turns out that Damascus is always good but only sometimes great. Tonight we went with the "just feed me" option, a set menu essentially, but this turned out to be something of a mistake. But that's on them, if you're making a set menu, then you should be putting your best foot forward. 



Don't just serve us a forgettable bean salad and call it a day. You can't maintain a 4 with that kind of behaviour, same problem with the olives. We love olives, but where's the good stuff that's supposed to come with it? Last time we were here, everything went with everything else, but this time, even though we were ordering off a set banquet menu, everything seemed so disjointed, the meal didn't gel together like some kind of cohesive whole. 



Bookings still recommended

We ate dinner here again in December 2023 



Pita breads 

Roast capsicum, leek, and walnut dip

Yoghurt and cracked wheat dip with cucumber and radish

Lamb shish

Fried potato with harra sauce

Fried cauliflower 

Candied pumpkin, orange kishta and walnut 



Welcome back, Damascus. After a very disappointing performance on platters night (see below), we were nervous to. be back. We wanted them to succeed, reaching the same lofty heights as the lunch when we first fell in love with them. And reach they did. 



A true joy of this meal was the way everything went with everything else. Any two items could be smushed together and taste delicious. We ate the two dips with everything else (except the dessert). We've previously used the term 'symphony of flavours' for Damascus' food, and this continues to be apt. The two dips in particular were outstanding, they were nothing like we were expecting (in fact, we didn't even know they were going to be dips), but in the very best way. 



The weakest link was the dessert, but we applaud them doing something different and authentic, perhaps it just wasn't quite to our tastes. 



Bookings recommended. Platters night (Tuesday) not recommended.

We ate dinner here in August 2023 



Vegetarian sharing platter 



What a catastrophic fall from grace. When we ate here just a week and a half earlier, this place blew our minds. You can imagine how excited we were to return for the famed platters night, we even invited Business Andy's mum. She cancelled at the last minute and after eating that meal, we're glad she missed it. 



Every Tuesday Damascus does sharing platter night, $100 for a platter for two ($110 for a platter with meat). The place was packed, the complete opposite of Saturday lunchtime. And therein lies the problem. Every person in that joint is ordering these platters, which are massive and contain many of the same elements regardless of which of the 3 platters you buy. Inevitably, this results in the kitchen cooking in bulk. Where our dishes at Saturday lunch had been lovingly crafted and super fresh, a lot of the platter had more of a "scoop some up and put it on the board" vibe. The flatbread was tepid. The cauliflower, the same cauliflower we enjoyed so much last time we were here, was flaccid. Last time each dish felt intricate and thoughtful, this time they'd popped on some fried spuds. Don't get us wrong, we love fried spuds and they were pretty good, but the comparison of the care and effort taken was night and day. 



Any time you order a platter, you run the risk of getting some things you don't like. For example, the Fee-Rex doesn't really jive with lentils or beans. These both featured on the platter, but even Business Andy, a lentil and bean enjoyer, thought they were pretty average. In a truly great restaurant, you can order things you wouldn't normally eat and you'll still like them. This was not the case here. And adding insult to injury, some of the best sounding items in the platter's description were MIA. 



We were just so very disappointed, this was nothing like our last meal here. We can still recommend eating at Damascus, just not the platters, and if you do want to go on a Tuesday night, bookings are essential.

We ate lunch here in July 2023



Deep fried cauliflower 

Roast pumpkin with peas and pomegranate 



It's Saturday lunchtime, and we're looking for somewhere to eat. The first 3 places we see are all completely full. Right next door is lucky number four, Damascus. And it's totally empty. This normally doesn't bode well. But Damascus isn't normal. It's extraordinary. An out-of-nowhere flavour powerhouse, we are huge fans. 



We often gravitate towards a fried cauliflower, but this one was totally different. It wasn't battered but instead just covered in sumac. It was crispy and really tasty with a superb dipping sauce. The pumpkin was a gamechanger. The bed of goodies it sat on, which was not just peas and pomegranate, was a whole symphony of delicious things, the names for which we can't remember. We were truly blown away.
