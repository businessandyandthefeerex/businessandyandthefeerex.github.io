Te Aro, Wellington



We ate dinner here in March 2024 



Tonkotsu Ramen 

Beef teriyaki 



Hey Ramen, get some better quality meat. When it comes to a simple dish like ramen, you can't skimp on the quality. Both the beef and pork were insanely dry, to the point we wondered whether the pork hadn't been cooked to order. The non-meat ingredients weren't doing much to pick up the slack. And only one piece of naruto, you tightwads.
