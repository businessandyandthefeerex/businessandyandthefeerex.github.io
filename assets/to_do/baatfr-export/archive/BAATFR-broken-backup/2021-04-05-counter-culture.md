We ate dinner here again in October 2023 



Fried corn, japapeno, and cheese nuggets

Fried tofu bites

Chips

Polenta chips



While the meals didn't light our souls on fire, you don't go to Counter Culture for the food. You go to play board games, like a pack of huge nerds. 



The meals did light our tongues on fire with their salt content. This was easily on our top 3 saltiest meals of all time list, maybe even top 1. We didn't have much choice as only snacks were available due to staffing, but you don't need to be a chef to know that a full cup and a half of salt per chip is a bit excessive. 



------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



We ate dinner here again in March 2023



Chicken burger and chips 

Macaroni cheese

Fried chicken gravy fries 



We were pleased to see that Counter Culture read our review from two years ago, and has removed both of the items we didn't like from the menu. In a cruel twist of fate, the new menu didn't fare any better. In fact, the burger was worse. The bun wasn't a bun, it was a section of ciabatta loaf. Wow. So dry, so chewy, so bready, so bad. Impossible to eat, way too tall, initial mouthfuls were bread only. It was clear the person who invented this burger was more concerned about appearance on the 'gram than flavour in the hand. 



The macaroni cheese was serviceable but kind of bland. They offered add ons, but the base meal should be able to stand alone, you shouldn't have to pay extra to get something that tastes good. The gravy fries were the highlight. While the chips were a little soggy, the gravy was flavourful and plentiful, welcomely full of mushrooms, and despite first appearances had a good ratio of gravy to fries. The chicken was nice enough, but we wouldn't go out of our way for it. 



----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



We ate lunch here in March 2021



Beef burger

Curly fries

Ice chocolate



While the food was average, the reason why you'd visit Counter Culture is for the board games. They have a great collection, and the staff were both knowledgeable and helpful. The curly fries were the short, crispy dregs of the packet, there were barely any big curlies at all. The burger was far too dry, perhaps they held the sauce back to help keep the board games clean. But if that's your reasoning, serve something that doesn't need sauce, like a quiche. Lastly, the ice chocolate was good, it had a big heckin scoop of ice cream which is exactly what we were hoping for. Bookings recommended.
