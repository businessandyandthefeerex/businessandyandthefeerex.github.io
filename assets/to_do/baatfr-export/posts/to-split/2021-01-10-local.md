Palmerston North



We ate lunch here again in February 2024



It's been a while, but we're back again. Local is just down the road from Business Andy's mum and is her favourite spot. We can see why. Coming in with another 3, the food was fresh and flavourful and the service very friendly. The sliders were fun, although they had cooked the halloumi a bit of a weird way, like it was battered? And perhaps the bread was on the dry side. The tacos were juicy and they gave you three of a good size. I think we'll be back again.

We ate breakfast here again in March 2021 



Poached egg on ciabatta with haloumi and potato hash

Scrambled eggs on multigrain with mushrooms, sausage, and black pudding

Golden milk latte



We really appreciated Local's build your own breakfasts, which start with eggs on toast and become as much or as little as you want. We both went for this option, but ended up with fairly different breakfasts. The sausage was really nice, as were the black pudding and the haloumi. The toast and the mushroom were fine, and the potato hash was a bit too dry and a bit too crisp. The eggs were all perfectly cooked but what stood out was that the poached egg was 100% dry. We hate it when someone drops a poached egg onto your toast that's still sopping wet from the pan, it ruins the toast. Plenty of breakfast joints do this, but not Local. The golden milk latte was unusual but nice. It doesn't contain any coffee and was instead made with milk tumeric, cinnamon, date puree, and black pepper.

We ate lunch here in January 2021 



Fried cauliflower with mayo and dukkah 

Fried chicken pieces with aioli 

Pita bread with hummus and pesto 



Local is a nice cafe with friendly service and they make delicious fried chicken. It wouldn't kill them to learn how to cut up a cauliflower though, as the fried cauliflower florets were huge and the batter rather flavourless. The pita was beautifully soft although the hummus and pesto were forgettable.
