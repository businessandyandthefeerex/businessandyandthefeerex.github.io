Big breakfast  
Breakfast muffin  
Banana smoothie  

The staff at Delish Cafe are delightful, they complimented Business Andy's shorts, and apologised for the wait even though we hadn't been waiting very long. 

The breakfast muffin was great, it was like an elevated version of a McMuffin. A juicy beef patty with melted cheese, tomato sauce, hash brown, and a fried egg in a toasted English muffin. It was magnificent, and very reasonably priced. The big breakfast was truly big, and was well executed. The hash browns deserve a special shout out, and perhaps the tomato could have been roasted a little longer, but the rest of the components were all really good. The banana smoothie was a real let down, they had doused the sides of the glass with banana syrup which ruined it.
