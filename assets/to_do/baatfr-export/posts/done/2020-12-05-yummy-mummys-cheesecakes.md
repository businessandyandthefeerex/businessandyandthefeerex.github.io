Caramel brownie slice  
Candy cane cheesecake  

Although best known for their cheesecake, Yummy Mummy's has a respectable cafe (cabinet food only). The vegetarian panini was unusual and interesting but overall very nice. It contained apple which was an eye opener, in a good way. The sandwich was fairly pedestrian but well-made and tasty. The caramel brownie slice was very caramel-y and delicious. The candy cane cheesecake was polarising - one of us found it literally inedible. The other found it rather good. Yummy Mummy's has a great range of cheesecake flavours, candy cane being a festive limited-time special.
