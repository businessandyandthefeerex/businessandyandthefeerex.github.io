Shake Out is a fast food burger establishment, with a difference. The set-up is designed to be streamlined with cashierless, cashless ordering using a tablet, a limited menu and no customisation. Speaking as people who sometimes like to remove the tomato from their burger, the lack of customisation was a little disappointing. However, the burgers were very good. Quality, fresh ingredients and great buns, these were simple burgers done well. The burgers are small which is great for lunch and was reflected in the price. The cheesy fries came with a cheese dipping sauce which was a bit heart attack-esque. The cheesecake shake was similarly artery clogging but we appreciated the unique cheesecake flavour, although it was a bit sweet and was very thick - we recommend mixing the shake thoroughly before drinking.
