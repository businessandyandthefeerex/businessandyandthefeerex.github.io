Singapore steak with rice  
Crispy pork massaman curry with rice  

Thai Orchid boasts the shortest time between ordering and meals arriving of anywhere we've reviewed to date, and that includes McDonalds and Burger King. The service was so fast at first we assumed they had brought the meals to the wrong table. The meals themselves were pretty good. The beef in the Singapore steak wasn't chewy or gristly, but unfortunately there wasn't enough sauce. The massaman was the opposite, plenty of delicious sauce but the meat was dry.
