We ate lunch here in February 2021



Margherita melt

BBQ melt



Warning: melt means large pizza bread. Two of these was way too much for us, one would have been about right. That said, they were both quite nice. There was plenty of mozzarella on the Margherita melt, and having cherry tomatoes instead of slices of regular tomatoes was the way to go. The BBQ melt was very meaty and hearty, and had plenty of sauce; a winning combo. On your arrival at Orlando Country Club, you'll notice the large sign detailing the dress code. No stubbies, no singlets, no hoodies, and presumably no facial tats. Who do they think they are? This isn't a real country club, it's just a driving range and cafe. And by the way, we saw all of these rules violated while we were there.
