We ate dinner here in July 2021



Cheeseburger with fries

Almighty Texan BBQ burger with a hash brown



When you look around at a McDonald's, you see a perfect cross section through humanity. Absolutely everybody eats at McDonald's. So well done, Ronald, on becoming a global sensation and appealing to the masses. Not so well done on the food. The meat was a bit dry across the board, the fries were fresh but unsalted, and the hash brown was old and stuck to its paper sleeve. The "almighty" Texan BBQ burger looked a bit sad and had half fallen over in it's box when it arrived via their new-to-us table service. This was probably thanks to the fried onion on the bottom, and although said onion did add a lot to the flavour of the burger, it took just as much away from its structural integrity. The cheeseburger was plain but inoffensive.
