Honey glazed beef hot plate with rice  
Mee goreng with BBQ pork  

If you want to read some classic manga, this is the place for you. If you want a nice meal, maybe you'd be better looking elsewhere. The beef was yuck, it was very chewy, and had that flavour you remember from that time you made that awful stir fry with the cheapest beef from Pak'nSave when you were first flatting. The sauce, while very sweet wasn't bad, and neither were the vegetables, but the beef ruined the whole thing. The mee goreng was reasonable, but couldn't elevate this meal above a 1 by itself.
