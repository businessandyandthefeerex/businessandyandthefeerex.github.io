We ate dinner here in June 2021 



Masala dosa

Cauliflower 65

Kerala paneer with rice 

Kerala duck masala with rice 

Kallappam 



Nicely done, Arranged Marriage. Elevating themselves compared to our last visit here, each one of these dishes is something we would be happy to order again. The standout was the kerala paneer, which was a flavour sensation. The kallappam was a fun very alternative to a naan, and was essentially a puffy pancake. The duck was on the bone and unfortunately the few stray bone bits that found their way into our mouths during the meal were a bit of an unpleasant surprise. The dosa, which is huge by the way, was a spectacle. A long thin crispy pancake rolled up with a soft spiced mash in the centre - yum yum. The cauliflower continued to be excellent.

We ate dinner here in January 2021



Mix platter: lentil doughnuts, fried cauliflower, fried chicken and dry fried beef 

South Indian butter chicken with rice

Potato and aubergine curry with rice

Plain, garlic, and garlic and cheese paratha



Arranged Marriage is not a typical Indian Restaurant. Many of the familiar curries are absent, and replaced by exotic fare such as dosa, grilled fish in banana leaf, and cauliflower Manchurian. Even naans are absent, replaced by the homologous paratha. While the food may be atypical, it is very nice. Of the paratha, we'd recommend the garlic above the other two. Of the mix platter starters the lentil doughnuts were the hero, while the dry fried beef could stay home if he wanted.
