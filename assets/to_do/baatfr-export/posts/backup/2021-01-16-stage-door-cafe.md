Bacon buttie and chips  
Macaroni cheese  

If the staff at Stage Door Cafe didn't want us to eat there, they should have just said. When we arrived (at 1:30pm on a Saturday) we were the only people there and while we were told they were still cooking, their faces said otherwise. The macaroni cheese was bland although we do commend their reheating skills - reheated cabinet mac'n'cheese often comes out as a brick-like structure, but this looked like it had just been made. The bacon buttie was very disappointing. For $15 we expect more than just bread and bacon hastily slapped together. There wasn't even any sauce in the sandwich.
