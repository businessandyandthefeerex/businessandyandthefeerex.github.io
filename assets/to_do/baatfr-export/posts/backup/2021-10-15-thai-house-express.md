We ate dinner here in October 2021



Tofu pad thai

Beef massaman curry with rice

Roti with satay sauce



Thai House Express, emphasis on the express. This is among the fastest food that we've been served, right up there with Wendy's. The roti was nice, but what has happened to the satay sauce? It wasn't peanutty at all, more like a watery curry sauce. Speaking of watery curry sauce, the massaman curry wasn't very nice at all. This dish is supposed to be rich and hearty, but it wasn't rich or hearty, and tasted far too strongly of fish sauce and sugar. In contrast, the pad thai was yummy, and we were pleased to see that we were served a tofu pad thai, not a vegetable pad thai featuring tofu.
