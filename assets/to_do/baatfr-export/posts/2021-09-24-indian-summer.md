We ate dinner here in September 2021



Shahi paneer with rice and plain naan

Lamb rajasthani with rice and cheese naan



The Fee-Rex has long held a conspiracy theory that multiple dishes on a typical Indian menu are actually the same thing. Indian Summer deepened this conspiracy. Tonight we ordered a shahi paneer, described as "cottage cheese cooked in rich cashew nut gravy and crushed almond." Sounds a bit like a korma, we thought. When it arrived at our table the waitress literally called it a korma. We knew it!! And it was a pale, bland korma at that. Similarly, Business Andy ordered a lamb rajasthani, descibed as "speciality of Rajsthan this unique beef or lamb dish cooked in chefâ€™s special sauce." Chef's special sauce my left nut. Unique my right nut. It was clearly a blend of Maggi tomato soup, some butter chicken, and sugar. We had the same exact dish at Indian2nite where it was called a makhani. The plain naan was pretty good, but the cheese naan was a nightmare. Half of it didn't even have any cheese at all, the other half had way too much, and it was mozzarella to boot.
