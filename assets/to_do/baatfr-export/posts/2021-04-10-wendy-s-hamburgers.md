We ate lunch here in April 2021



1/4 lb beef burger regular meal

Chicken pretzel pub burger



This meal was a real mix of fresh highs and cheesy lows. The beef burger was very good, fresh ingredients, and a great ratio of bun:salad:sauce:meat. The fries were fresh, crispy, thicccccc, and neither over nor under salted. They had a pump your own sauce station, which was a great throwback to the 90's, and the L&P was not watered down. The chicken burger was a tragedy. The culprit: a foul beer cheese sauce, which tasted like neither beer nor cheese. The burger was sopping with it, completely obscuring all other flavours. It is hard to believe that anybody would enjoy that sauce, and nobody would enjoy that quantity of it. The pretzel pub range is for a limited time only, thankfully.
