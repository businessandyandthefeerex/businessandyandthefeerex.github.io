Rustic Steakhouse

Reviews  ·  25 January 2021  ·  New Zealand › Manawatu › Taihape

Rating: 3

We ate lunch here in January 2021.

Eye fillet, polenta chips and BBQ sauce
Beef burger with chips

Rustic Steak House does simple food, but does it pretty well. The burger was basic but tasty. Particularly welcome was the steak knife through the middle of the burger, allowing the burger to be cut in half making it a breeze to eat. The eye fillet was properly cooked and came with the very welcome surprise of a half clove of roasted garlic. A little side salad wouldn't have gone amiss.
