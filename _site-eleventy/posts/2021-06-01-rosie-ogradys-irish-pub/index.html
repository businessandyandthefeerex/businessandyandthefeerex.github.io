Rosie O'Grady's Irish Pub

Reviews  ·  01 June 2021  ·  New Zealand › Manawatu › Palmerston North

Rating: 3

We ate dinner here in June 2021.

Lamb pot pie with garlic bread
"Mor burger" (double beef) with chips

Rosie O'Grady's is a typical Irish pub, cosy fires, walls covered in Irish paraphernalia, Guinness on tap, and offering typical Irish pub fare. The meals were basic but done well. The pot pie was very hearty, no gristly bits or unpleasant surprises in the stew with a crispy pastry top and nice thin garlic bread. The burger was pretty full on, with two large patties and was too big to be eaten with hands. It came with a couple of homemade beer battered onion rings that were much appreciated.
