Hungry Horse Cafe

Reviews  ·  29 January 2022  ·  New Zealand › Manawatu › Palmerston North

Rating: 2

We ate lunch here in January 2022.

Smashed avo on toast with a poached egg
Baked potato with mince

We'll talk about these meals in the order that they arrived.

The smashed avo on toast was middling, the toast itself was thick, soft, and fresh. It came with a very welcome slice of lemon, which paired well with the avocado itself. Unfortunately, the poached egg was underdone and a bit sloppy. And extra unfortunately, there was a huge piece of lettuce under the avo, on the toast. We really don't know who wants this, it was entirely unnecessary.

This space represents the gap between us finishing the avo on toast, and the arrival of the baked potato.

The potato itself was not underdone, but lacked fluffiness. We carefully avoided the vegetarian mince option because it contained red beans and chickpeas, so we were quite upset when the beef mince arrived with both red beans and chickpeas. Admittedly these both didn't taste like much, but that's not a compliment. Could have used a little more sour cream, extra points for the sweet chilli sauce on the top.
