Noodles & Dumplings

Reviews  ·  09 April 2021  ·  New Zealand › Manawatu › Palmerston North

Rating: 3

We ate dinner here in April 2021.

  • Spring onion pancake
  • Fried thin noodles with BBQ pork
  • Fried fish with salt & pepper
  • Fried vegetable dumplings

Friends of ours used Noodles & Dumplings to cater their wedding. We can see why. The food ranged from good to delicious, the people working there were warm and friendly, and we really appreciated their eclectic collection of mufti outfits. Best dressed male wore a dope denim jacket with a bumblebee on the back, best dressed female was in a crop top and high waisted super short shorts. The spring onion pancake was the weakest offering of the bunch, but it was by no means bad, and it was a three way tie for the top spot. The Fee-Rex doesn't much like dumplings, but she was popping these ones like Tic Tacs, they were just delicious. The fish had an excellent batter, and the fish itself was real fillets, not the hoki composite BS you get at some other places, not naming any names as we're not in the business of kicking Thai Chef's Restaurant when they are down. The noodles were flavourful and there was a lot of them, a winning combo. We ordered way too much food for two people, but fortunately they had takeout boxes on hand as they were doing a roaring takeaway trade. FYI you order and pay at the counter before sitting down.
