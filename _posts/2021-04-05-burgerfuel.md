Bacon backfire burger: grilled chicken, brie, bacon, lettuce, red onion, tomato  
Low rider burger: crispy fried chicken, caramelised onion, cheese, lettuce, red onion, tomato  

There's not much to say about Burgerfuel. It's among the better burger places, we certainly would choose it over Burger King, no disrespect to the King himself. The burgers were both flavourful and well constructed, although both came with tomato after we asked for none. Shout out to the Doofer, it must be aggressively trademarked otherwise everyone would be copying it.
