The Pensioner Pack: fish, chips, tartare sauce  
Fish and cheese burger  

Thirty years of zero upkeep on the quintessential 80's decor has taken its toll, the result is a bit depressing. Not as depressing as the fish burger though. The burger tasted like a combination of sugar, oil, and nothing, and not in a good way. The fish in the Pensioner Pack was rubbery, although the slice of lemon was appreciated, and the chips were alright.
