Nicely done, Arranged Marriage. Elevating themselves compared to our last visit here, each one of these dishes is something we would be happy to order again. The standout was the kerala paneer, which was a flavour sensation. The kallappam was a fun very alternative to a naan, and was essentially a puffy pancake. The duck was on the bone and unfortunately the few stray bone bits that found their way into our mouths during the meal were a bit of an unpleasant surprise. The dosa, which is huge by the way, was a spectacle. A long thin crispy pancake rolled up with a soft spiced mash in the centre - yum yum. The cauliflower continued to be excellent.
