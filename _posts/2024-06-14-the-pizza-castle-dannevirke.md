Italian pizza (salami, ham, olives, mushroom, onion)  
Cheesy jalapeno bites  

Pizza Castle? Pizza dungeon more like it. Well, maybe that's a bit harsh, it wasn't damp or dark. But it was cold and bad stuff happened here. 

Beside having seating for 14, Pizza Castle gave no other indication that dine-in was even an option. There was no cutlery, no water, no plates, not a single serviette in sight. We ate from the box. 

The bites were basically super salty crumbed balls of low-flavour cheese with tiny bits of jalapeno in them along with some unwelcome corn. They were not spicy, nor were they flavourful. They needed a dipping sauce desperately, but there was none on hand. And the pizza had a reasonable base, but the toppings were very bland.
