Roast eggplant  

Chow has a great menu. While it's our first time reviewing, it's not our first time dining here, and we reckon you could come back time after time, ordering different things and consistently being pleased. And don't even get us started on the cocktail menu, which is both inventive and delicious, and has a propensity for 2-for-1-specials. 

The $17 lunch special is great value. The pho, curry, moneybags and rice were all for $17. Plus they accidentally bought us a second rice then let us keep it. We appreciate this generosity (and it makes us scowl at The Botanist, check out their review if you're not sure what we mean). The curry was slightly boring, and had those baby corns, which we suggest that everybody hates. Otherwise it was all good, and the moneybags were great. 

The roast eggplant was bloody spicy without warning, which was a bit alarming. Also, it was impossible to eat with the cutlery provided (one of those big ceramic spoons for eating pho, and chopsticks). 

That's a lot of complaining about a meal that we really enjoyed. But we only complain because on another day, they could be a 4. We look forward to returning.
