While not quite at a four, Little Savannah continues to excel. And their business shows this, with the place being packed out yet again. We were also impressed by their cocktails, we tried five of their plethora of flavoured espresso martinis, and there wasn’t a clanger in the bunch. 

Also clanger-less was the meals, with the possible exception of the biltong. This was hard to judge - The Fee-Rex hasn’t had biltong in 15+ years and doesn’t really like jerky, so perhaps her opinion doesn’t count for much. But even Business Andy thought it was lacking in flavour, was just a general boring beefy vibe which wasn’t necessarily worth having. 

What was worth having was the pork belly flatbread, which was remarkably good. The way they had incorporated just enough crackling in each piece of pork to have a fun crunch, without it becoming a hard-to-chew crackling-fest, that’s true skill. 

Speaking of true skill, Little Savannah has killed it with yet another salad. We can’t speak highly enough of this. Many restaurants consider a salad to be a throwaway option. You almost feel punished for ordering one - we almost feel like the thought process is something along the lines of “oh you’re getting a salad are you, well screw you, if you won’t eat a ‘real’ main then you’ll get what you’re given”. But not here. This salad was perfectly balanced, every mouthful was special, there wasn’t a period where you had to slog through too much lettuce. It was perfection. And finally, the pasta was good but not great, had it been a bit better, their rating could have reached the highest level that we award.
