Vegetable okonomiyaki  
Teriyaki beef donburi  

Although the decor was dated and the food service was slow, Hanamizuki serves good food. The okonomiyaki was nommy with a good combination of sauces. The donburi vegetables were fresh, the meat tender. The sesame on the spinach was a standout.
