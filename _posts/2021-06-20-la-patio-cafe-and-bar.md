Pulled duck burger with poutine  
Mongolian beef salad  

Holy duck, what a burger. It was extremely juicy, flavourful, and unusual. We didn't expect a middling hotel restaurant to push the boat out like this. The poutine was delicious, and it was so nice to have something that wasn't just chips and tomato sauce on the side. The salad was boring and frankly there was nothing Mongolian about it. It's a pity that the salad couldn't keep up with the burger, La Patio's rating was really dragged down by some dead Mongolian weight. When you visit La Patio, be sure to keep an ear out for their instrumental cover tracks, which ranged from Radiohead to Shawn Mendes, to The Beatles. Guessing what song was being covered was a fun challenge, not to brag, but we got them all except one.
