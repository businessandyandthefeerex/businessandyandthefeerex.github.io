Fried noodles, cabbage, red cabbage, red onion, carrot, garlic aioli, sweet chilli, tofu, and Singapore Steak  
Roast potato, cabbage, red cabbage, carrot, pickles, red onion, cheese, sesame sauce, and won tons  

This place is sort of like Subway, in that you choose a base (rice, potato, noodles), salads, sauces, and meats. Very popular, and frankly, we can see why. Tasty, well priced, lots of options. The potatoes were a little average, the tofu was a bit sloppy on the inside, and overall the meals were very salty, but they were very yummy. Lunch only.
