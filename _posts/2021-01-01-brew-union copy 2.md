Philly cheese steak burger with chips  
Lamb rack with potato and pumpkin gratin, pea puree, and yoghurt harissa sauce  
Ginger kiss cocktail  
Salted caramel martini  

The cocktails continue to impress, the ginger kiss was a little different this time around, we think they had added limoncello, and it wasn't quite as good as we remembered. The lamb rack was perfectly cooked, and the gratin was delicious, it really stood out. It was unique, they had layered the potato and pumpkin together in the one gratin, yum yum. The Philly cheese steak burger wasn't quite as good as the other burgers that we've had here, but was still very strong, and there wasn't too much cheese in it.
