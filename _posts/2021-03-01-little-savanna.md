Crumbed goats' cheese balls  
BBQ pork belly flatbread  
Beef burger with added blue cheese butter and cheese, chips and onion rings  
Pork belly, pumpkin and potato mash, cabbage and bacon  

Little Savannah continues to impress, although perhaps not quite to the level of our lunch last year. The pork belly flatbread was delicious, especially the generous amounts of crackling. The burger was tasty, but juicy to the point of being wet causing severe bun deterioration. The cabbage and bacon accompanying the pork belly was delicious and there was a considerable amount of crackling, although the meat itself and the mash were a bit plain.  Bookings still essential.
