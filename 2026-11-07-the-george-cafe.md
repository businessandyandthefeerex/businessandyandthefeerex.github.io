Outside of Levin you'll find Speldhurst Estate. It's a retirement community that has been built up over the last decade or so. And while you have to be 65+ to buy a house at Speldhurst, you don't have to be 65+ to dine. On a rainy Saturday afternoon, yes there were plenty of grey heads, but we did spy two dining tables without a single apparent retiree (unless grandma be keepin it very, very tight), and there wasn't a what-are-you-doing-here energy when we came in off the street. The dining area is large, the menu options crowd-pleasing European retiree fare with very generous portions. 

The fish and chips did a respectable job. The fish was quality gurnard and had been well fried with a generous side of tartare sauce. The chips were absolutely fine, and the salad was passable on the scale of side salads, many of which are disastrous. The loaded wedges had unfortunately been overloaded. Don't get us wrong, under-loading is a serious crime in wedgeworld. But over-loading is no joke, especially when you're plowing through huge globs of valuemetric edam cheese without a wedge in sight.
